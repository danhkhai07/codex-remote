How tracking and averages work

Every day has one total. Editing it adds an adjustment while automatic tracking continues. Use Bắt đầu tính / Dừng to track a shared timed session. Activity timestamps from all local active and archived Codex sessions are combined into one timeline. Gaps up to 60 minutes count as work, allowing for reading and switching conversations. Longer gaps are excluded in full. Time after the latest activity is counted only if another activity arrives within the allowance; simply leaving a session open does not keep adding hours. Each minute counts once, and intervals crossing midnight are split in Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh time. These are estimates: the system cannot distinguish reading from a short break or assistant execution. Days with no recorded evidence remain unknown; later activity continues adding to your adjusted total.

Averages divide recorded hours by days with recorded hours, including zero-hour days. Unknown dates are excluded. The 7-day and 30-day averages use complete days before today. “Last month” means the last 30 complete days; the chart includes today in its 7/30-day ranges and all history; previous calendar month remains historical. All-time means available local history and includes today’s partial total.

Connected dashboards synchronize the shared timer and daily totals every 5 seconds. Generated estimates and vault logs refresh every 15 minutes. A standalone downloaded HTML file is a read-only snapshot.

Use the daily-hours form to adjust your total, or enter 0 for a day off. All saves go to the shared server file. CSV files are exports; importing a CSV through this dashboard updates the common totals.
